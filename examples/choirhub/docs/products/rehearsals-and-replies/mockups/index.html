ChoirHub · milestone 1

Rehearsals and replies

Eight screens as they would look on a phone, built from the design system we agreed. These are pictures: nothing you tap does anything, and no data here is real. Your phone's light or dark setting is what you'll see; both were designed.

Walk them in this order

First as a singer — Sign in, then My rehearsals, then Rehearsal — because that is the part the whole thing depends on. Then as yourself: Rehearsals, New rehearsal, the paste step, and Who is coming. Singers last; it is the thin list that makes the rest possible.

How a singer gets in: an email link, no password. Also what a stranger sees.

New rehearsal · paste a message

The paste step end to end: thinking, what I understood, and what happens when it fails.

Who is coming

The screen you open the evening before. Names, three groups, contact details one tap away.

Singers

The choir list: name, email, section. Thin on purpose.

Three things I'd like your reaction on

1. The singer's one tap

The two buttons sit on the list itself, so answering never needs a second screen. The detail screen repeats them. Right trade, or would you rather the list stayed a list?

2. Who is coming

No answer yet comes first, because it is the only group you can act on, and phone numbers are one tap behind a name. Is that the screen you want open the evening before?

3. The paste step

"Here is what I understood" shows everything before saving and marks what it guessed. Is that enough to trust it weekly, or too much fuss?

Approving these means the build must match them, screen by screen. Anything you want different is cheaper to change now than at any later point.
