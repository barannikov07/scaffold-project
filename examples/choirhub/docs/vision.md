# Vision: ChoirHub

The north star. Every assessment and every PRD is checked against this document. It may change
as we learn, but only through a recorded decision (see the changelog at the bottom), never by
quietly drifting.

Status: first draft, written from the scaffold interview on 2026-09-16. Sasha reacts to it; it is
not settled until she does.

## The end state

When this is finished and working perfectly, this is what exists:

Every rehearsal exists in ChoirHub before anyone thinks to ask about it: date, place, and the
songs the choir will work on that night. Singers open it on their phone, see what is coming and
what to prepare, and say "coming" or "not coming" in one tap. The evening before, the conductor
opens one screen and knows exactly who will be there, without counting a single WhatsApp reply
and without chasing anybody. Who actually turned up is recorded as it happens and is never
rewritten afterwards, so the choir has an honest history of itself. Membership fees are paid and
reconciled in the same place, so the admin evening that used to eat a Sunday simply disappears.
And because the history is honest, ChoirHub can quietly tell the conductor that a singer who used
to come every week has missed the last three — while there is still time to send the message that
keeps them in the choir.

## Who it is for

Community choirs run by one person who is a musician first and an administrator second.

- **The conductor** (the user who matters most). Runs everything today: the WhatsApp group, the
  list of who is coming, the register, the fees. Every hour ChoirHub gives back is an hour of
  music.
- **The singers.** Adults with day jobs and full evenings. They will not learn an app. They open a
  link, see their own choir life, tap once, and get on with their day.
- **Section leaders**, later. Sasha mentioned them as a maybe; nothing is built for them yet.

## Principles that settle trade-offs

When two good options conflict, these decide. Three to five, each one line.

1. The app does the tedious part; the person keeps the decision. *(suggested by Claude from the
   interview — react to this one in particular)*
2. A singer's answer takes one tap on a phone at a tram stop; if it takes more, the singer is
   right and we are wrong.
3. History is added to, never rewritten: what happened is a record, not a field.
4. A singer sees their own life in the choir; the conductor sees the choir. Contact details
   belong to the person, not to the group.
5. Nothing that matters lives only in a WhatsApp thread.

## What we will not do

Explicit non-goals. A feature that needs one of these is Later or Dropped unless the vision is
amended.

- Replace WhatsApp as the place the choir chats. The chatter stays there; ChoirHub keeps the facts.
- Show singers each other's phone numbers or email addresses, ever, in any view or export.
- Become a sheet-music or recording library. *(suggested: an obvious temptation, and a different
  product — react if you disagree)*
- Become a public concert website with ticket sales. *(suggested)*
- Serve many choirs at once before this one choir is run well by it. *(suggested)*

## How we know we have won

Two or three observable signs, not metrics for their own sake.

- Before a rehearsal the conductor opens one screen instead of scrolling the group, and believes
  what it says.
- A whole term passes without a single "who is coming tomorrow?" message in the WhatsApp group.
- The conductor can name the singers who are drifting away, and contacted them while they were
  still in the choir.
